# Unit 03: The Math Behind Neural Networks

## Overview

This unit delves into the mathematical foundations that power neural networks and deep learning. It covers tensor operations, linear algebra, calculus, and the mathematical constructs essential for understanding and implementing deep learning algorithms.

## Topics Covered

1. How does a neural network look like?
2. The matrix magic
3. Visualizing deep learning
4. The elephant in the room
5. Programmatic expression of deep learning's math constructs
6. Operations with tensors
7. Array broadcasting
8. Scalar product and inner product of tensors
9. Morphing shapes of tensors
10. Gradient calculation

## Learning Objectives

By the end of this unit, students will be able to:

- Understand the mathematical structure of neural networks
- Perform tensor operations essential for deep learning computations
- Apply matrix operations to implement neural network forward and backward passes
- Utilize array broadcasting for efficient computation
- Compute scalar and inner products of tensors
- Manipulate tensor shapes through reshaping and transposition
- Calculate gradients using calculus and computational graph techniques
- Translate mathematical formulations into programmatic implementations
- Visualize and interpret mathematical operations in neural networks
